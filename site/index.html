How it works

Three ideas, and that is the whole app.

🗺️

Walk the trail

Ten areas per course, drawn as places — Tidepool Flats, Cell City, Exam Summit. Each stop is a short set of questions on that unit's real topics. Clear the stops, face the boss, the next area opens.

🎯

It notices what you miss

Every answer is recorded against its topic. The practice tab ranks your weakest categories from what you have actually answered — and the drill behind each one asks about that topic, not the course at large.

🔥

Come back tomorrow

A streak for turning up, gems for finishing, and a companion whose ability changes how a session plays. Cleared an area? The endless review at its foot keeps going as long as you want it to.

Bring someone with you

Twelve companions, each with one ability that actually does something. Sixty bosses to get past.

About the questions
